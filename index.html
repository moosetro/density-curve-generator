About the mathematics

This tool uses the skew-normal family: f(x) = 2φ(z)Φ(αz)/ω, where z = (x − ξ)/ω. Set α = 0 for an ordinary normal curve. The shape slider α is not the standardized skewness γ₁; γ₁ is reported above and ranges approximately from −0.967 to +0.967 here.

With δ = α/√(1 + α²), the underlying scale is ω = σ/√(1 − 2δ²/π) and location is ξ = μ − ωδ√(2/π). These adjustments keep E[X] = μ and SD[X] = σ exactly. Probabilities and quantiles are numerical approximations. The graph may omit very small tail areas, but the full distribution has total area 1. All code, styles, and calculations are included in this file; no internet connection is needed.
